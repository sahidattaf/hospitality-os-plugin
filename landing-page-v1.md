## Headline

**Fewer missed details. More time on the plate, not the inbox.**

*An AI operating system for hospitality teams — with your approval before anything is sent or confirmed.*

## The problem

- Catering and event requests arrive scattered across WhatsApp, email and phone calls, often missing the date, the headcount or the location.
- Allergy and dietary details get lost between the first message and the kitchen.
- Owners and managers spend evenings answering questions that a good checklist could have gathered up front.

## The solution

- **Gathers what's missing.** The assistant asks for date and time, service mode, location, menu and (optionally) budget, so each request is complete before you see it.
- **Flags allergies for kitchen review.** Dietary needs are surfaced to a person, never silently assumed.
- **You stay in control.** It does not quote prices, confirm bookings or contact anyone on its own. Nothing goes out without owner approval.
