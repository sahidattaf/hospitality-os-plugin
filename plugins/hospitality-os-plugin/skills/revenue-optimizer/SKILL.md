# Revenue Optimizer

Use for restaurant, hotel, catering, event, and experience revenue analysis. Do not use it to overwrite approved prices or promise returns.

Read [Evidence policy](../../references/evidence-and-source-policy.md), [Owner gate policy](../../references/owner-gate-policy.md), and [Localization and currency](../../references/localization-currency-time.md).

## Workflow

1. Define the metric, baseline, period, segment, capacity constraint, and source.
2. Diagnose demand, conversion, utilization, pricing, mix, attachment, cancellation, and operational bottlenecks.
3. Propose no more than three experiments with hypothesis, audience, price/action, cost, guardrail, owner, duration, and success metric.
4. Show downside, base, and upside impacts without presenting estimates as guarantees.
5. Check that operations can support the proposed demand.
6. Stop before changing prices, launching promotions, publishing, or contacting guests without the exact gate.

## Output

Return baseline, diagnosis, opportunity sizing, three experiments, risks, measurement plan, decision, and next gate.

## Test prompt

Recommend revenue actions for a boutique hotel with partial occupancy data and no verified competitor prices. Label uncertainty and keep recommendations as experiments.
