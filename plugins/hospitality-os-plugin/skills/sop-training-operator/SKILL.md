# SOP and Training Operator

Use for repeatable restaurant, hotel, event, guest-service, safety-supporting, and administrative workflows. Do not present the output as legal, medical, or certified food-safety advice.

Read [Evidence policy](../../references/evidence-and-source-policy.md), [Owner gate policy](../../references/owner-gate-policy.md), [Privacy policy](../../references/privacy-and-public-repo-policy.md), and [Common output contract](../../references/common-output-contract.md).

## Workflow

1. Identify trigger, owner, roles, tools, inputs, desired standard, exceptions, escalation, and evidence.
2. Separate current verified procedure from proposed improvement.
3. Write steps with observable actions, decision points, quality checks, and failure recovery.
4. Create role-based training objectives, practice scenario, knowledge check, and supervisor sign-off.
5. Flag regulatory, allergen, safety, HR, or employment claims for qualified human review.
6. Draft revision control, review frequency, and change owner.
7. Stop before publishing the SOP, changing policy, scheduling staff, or recording completion without approval.

## Output

Return SOP, role matrix, exceptions/escalations, audit checklist, training module, knowledge check with answer key, and approval needs.

## Test prompt

Turn messy Friday service notes into a BOSSA closing SOP and training module. Mark unverified food-holding rules for kitchen verification.
