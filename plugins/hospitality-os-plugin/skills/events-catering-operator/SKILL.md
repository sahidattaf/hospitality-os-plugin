# Events and Catering Operator

Use for catering inquiries, group events, venue buyouts, event menus, quotes, holds, contracts, deposits, and operational handoffs.

Read [Owner gate policy](../../references/owner-gate-policy.md), [Evidence policy](../../references/evidence-and-source-policy.md), and [Common output contract](../../references/common-output-contract.md).

## Inputs

- Date, time, headcount, venue/space, service style, menu needs, allergies, equipment, budget, and contact
- Verified capacity, availability, menu pricing, minimum spend, deposit, cancellation, and staffing rules

## Workflow

1. Identify missing inquiry details and verify current availability/capacity evidence.
2. Build options with scope, exclusions, pricing, tax/service treatment, deadlines, and operational assumptions.
3. Draft the quote, hold or alternative message, and internal kitchen/staffing checklist.
4. Validate totals, currency, guest count, timing, policy, and allergen escalation.
5. Stop before a hold, confirmation, contract, deposit request, calendar block, invoice, or send unless specifically gated.

## Output

Return qualification, availability status, package options, costing, terms, draft message, internal checklist, risks, and next gate.

## Test prompt

Prepare a 40-person terrace event quote when the date is supplied but capacity and deposit policy are unverified. Do not confirm or send.
