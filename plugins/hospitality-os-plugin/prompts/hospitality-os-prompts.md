# Hospitality OS Prompt Starters

## Command formula

State the Skill, business, outcome, source of truth, allowed actions, prohibited actions, validation, and stop condition.

> Use **[skill]** for **[business]**. Outcome: **[result]**. Source: **[authoritative source]**. You may **[allowed actions]**. Do not **[prohibited actions]**. Validate **[evidence/tests]**. Stop after **[boundary]**.

## Starters

- Use `hospitality-command-center` to prepare this week’s verified BOSSA owner review. Do not update Notion.
- Use `hospitality-sales-operator` to qualify this hotel and draft outreach. Verify the recipient; do not send.
- Use `events-catering-operator` to prepare this 40-person event quote. Do not hold the date or request a deposit.
- Use `guest-sales-reservations` to draft a response and alternatives. Do not confirm or change a booking.
- Use `guest-experience-operator` to draft a recovery response. Do not publish, refund, or promise compensation.
- Use `menu-product-operator` to cost this item from the master record. Do not edit or publish the menu.
- Use `inventory-forecast-operator` to forecast weekend stock and draft an order plan. Do not place an order.
- Use `revenue-optimizer` to design three measurable pricing experiments. Do not change live prices.
- Use `hospitality-market-intelligence` to research current competitors with citations. Do not contact competitors.
- Use `hospitality-delivery-manager` to plan this pilot with synthetic data. Stop before client access or deployment.
- Use `sop-training-operator` to turn these notes into an SOP and training module. Flag unverified safety rules.
- Use `video-production-operator` to create the multilingual draft package. Stop before production, scheduling, or publication.
