# Controlled Hospitality OS Examples

All examples are synthetic. They demonstrate draft behavior and do not assert current prices, availability, policies, capacities, menu contents, or performance.

## Command center

Prepare a weekly owner review from a supplied synthetic dataset. One KPI conflicts with its source. Report the conflict and do not update any dashboard.

## Sales

Qualify a boutique hotel lead and draft outreach. The contact has an ambiguous name. Request identity confirmation and do not send.

## Events and catering

Prepare two package options for a synthetic 40-person event. Capacity and deposit policy are missing. Flag them and do not hold or confirm the date.

## Reservations

Draft a warm alternative-time response for a synthetic BOSSA inquiry. Do not claim availability or confirmation without a current reservation source and owner gate.

## Guest experience

Draft a private recovery reply for a synthetic slow-service complaint. Offer options only, without promising a refund, credit, or public response.

## Menu and product

Model a proposed Fire Box using owner-supplied counts and costs. Label it as a concept and do not change `BOSSA Menu Items — Master`.

## Inventory

Forecast a synthetic weekend ingredient need using base, low, and high cases. Prepare an order plan but do not place it.

## Revenue

Design three measurable experiments from supplied occupancy or menu-mix evidence. Do not change live rates or prices.

## Market intelligence

Compare public hospitality offers with citations and matching dates, taxes, inclusions, and currencies. Do not contact competitors.

## Delivery

Plan a four-week pilot with synthetic data, acceptance criteria, and rollback. Stop before client access or production deployment.

## SOP and training

Convert synthetic service notes into an SOP and knowledge check. Route unverified food-safety or employment rules to a qualified human.

## Video production

Create a multilingual draft package with verified facts and documented identity consent. Scheduling and publication remain separate gated stages.
