# Owner Gate Policy

Hospitality OS defaults to research, analysis, planning, and drafting. A completed draft does not authorize execution.

Require a fresh, explicit owner gate immediately before:

- Sending email, WhatsApp, review replies, proposals, confirmations, or outreach
- Creating, changing, cancelling, or waitlisting a reservation or event
- Blocking a calendar, issuing a contract, requesting a deposit, refunding, discounting, or purchasing
- Changing a menu price, production record, inventory record, guest record, or access setting
- Publishing or scheduling a website, menu, image, social post, video, or case study

Parse each gate into the exact project, action, target, system, allowed changes, prohibited changes, validation, and stop condition. Perform only that scope. Verify every material result individually and report what was not performed.

If no gate exists, produce a complete draft and stop before the mutation.
