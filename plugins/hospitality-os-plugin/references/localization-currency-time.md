# Localization, Currency, and Time

- Default to English. When the owner begins in Papiamentu or Spanish, lead with Papiamentu and provide the requested bilingual support.
- Localize meaning and hospitality tone; do not translate word-for-word.
- Use Curaçao local time (`America/Curacao`) unless another timezone is explicit.
- Use XCG as the primary operating currency. Add USD only as a labeled reference when useful.
- State the exchange-rate source, rate, and date for conversions. Do not silently convert ANG, XCG, or USD.
- Preserve metric units and clearly label portions, yields, occupancy, covers, keys, rooms, and percentages.
