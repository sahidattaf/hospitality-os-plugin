# BOSSA Marketing & Content

Read [operating boundaries](references/operating-boundaries.md) and [source navigation](references/source-navigation.md) before producing material recommendations. Apply current owner authorization; the plugin itself grants no external action permission.

## Inputs

Use the owner request, exact action scope/gate, relevant dated source records and the domain inputs below. Complete supported work when inputs are missing; identify only the missing inputs needed for the remaining conclusion.

## Procedure and output

1. Identify goal, audience (local/tourist), channel, language, format and current phase. Retrieve approved menu item, offer, availability, CTA/contact, evidence of prices and authentic asset rights/provenance.
2. Build an owner-review package: hook, copy, CTA, asset list, sequence, captions and measurement plan. Avoid implying live service/opening when readiness is unverified. Translate naturally in EN/PAP/NL/ES while preserving approved prices and terms.
3. For video identify actual source generations/files, voice track, sound bed and missing material before assembly. Use supplied authentic BOSSA food/location assets when required; never present AI-generated scenes as real BOSSA. Use clear placeholders for missing footage. Prepare private drafts within the authorized platform/project only.
4. For YouTube supply title, description, verified links, tags, thumbnail brief and playlist order/selection. Distinguish private owner review from publishing; preserve exact publication gate.
5. Define measurement using verified impressions, clicks, leads and attributed conversions; separate targets from actual performance. Use current sources for competitor or platform claims and cite them.

Return: complete reviewable campaign/video packet; asset provenance and missing files; approved versus proposed offer fields; channel QA; exact publish/send gate and next action. Do not publish, spend, contact partners or activate an offer without the matching authorization.
