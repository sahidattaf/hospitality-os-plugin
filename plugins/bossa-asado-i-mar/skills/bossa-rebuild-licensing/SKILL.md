# BOSSA Rebuild & Licensing

Read [operating boundaries](references/operating-boundaries.md) and [source navigation](references/source-navigation.md) before producing material recommendations. Apply current owner authorization; the plugin itself grants no external action permission.

## Inputs

Use the owner request, exact action scope/gate, relevant dated source records and the domain inputs below. Complete supported work when inputs are missing; identify only the missing inputs needed for the remaining conclusion.

## Procedure and output

1. Read the Rebuild, Licensing and Document registers and actual evidence. Classify each item as supported, incomplete, conflicting or missing. Record issue dates, scope and issuer only from documents.
2. Use P0-R1 checklist: exterior, entrance, dining, kitchen, fire station, extraction route, electrical panel, plumbing/drainage, bathrooms, walls/doors, exits, storage and signage. Require measured dimensions, date, surveyor, photos, current sketch and written report. Never estimate dimensions from photos.
3. Sequence P0-R1 survey → P0-R2 measured drawings → room/workstream scope. Assess grill, kitchen, extraction, electrical, plumbing, hygiene, egress, equipment, finishes and signage with their documented dependencies.
4. Prepare P0-L1 permitted-use/building history, P0-L2 previous horeca license and P0-L3 alcohol/category questions. Treat historical butcher/supermarket use as history, not restaurant authorization. Never select a legal category or certify compliance from a concept.
5. For legal requirements or current official procedure, browse authoritative Curaçao sources and cite them; distinguish published guidance from professional/authority confirmation. Do not contact authorities, submit forms or commission contractors unless explicitly authorized.

Return: gap matrix (item, evidence/date, status, missing document, owner, dependency, next action); survey collection checklist or scoped contractor brief; exact opening gate. Stop readiness conclusions where professional design/inspection, permit evidence or owner scope is missing.
