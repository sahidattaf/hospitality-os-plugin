# BOSSA skill routing

- `$bossa-command-center` — BOSSA Owner Command Center: Triage BOSSA owner requests, daily priorities, cross-workstream reviews and opening readiness into one evidence-based management answer.
- `$bossa-rebuild-licensing` — BOSSA Rebuild & Licensing: Review BOSSA Phase 0 survey, rebuild dependencies, licensing evidence and opening-readiness gaps; prepare contractor and authority question packets.
- `$bossa-menu-costing` — BOSSA Menu & Costing: Cost BOSSA recipes, breads and Fire Boxes from the authoritative workbook and menu master; reconcile units, yields, item status and proposed margins.
- `$bossa-fire-kitchen` — BOSSA Fire & Kitchen: Prepare BOSSA bread, Fire Box, grill, sauce and Sour Bar test plans, prep sheets and recipe approval packets from real kitchen evidence.
- `$bossa-inventory-suppliers` — BOSSA Inventory & Suppliers: Review BOSSA stock evidence, ingredient purchasing, reorder scenarios and supplier setup; prepare purchase proposals without inventing inventory or ordering.
- `$bossa-guests-reservations` — BOSSA Guests & Reservations: Handle BOSSA guest questions, WhatsApp conversion, reservation requests, complaints and loyalty drafts using verified availability and business facts.
- `$bossa-catering-events` — BOSSA Catering & Events: Qualify BOSSA party and catering leads, propose menus for costing, calculate delivery/packaging scenarios and prepare owner-review quote packets.
- `$bossa-marketing-content` — BOSSA Marketing & Content: Build BOSSA multilingual campaigns, Fire Box promotions, social posts and authentic YouTube/InVideo briefs with verified offers and publication gates.
- `$bossa-finance-analytics` — BOSSA Finance & Analytics: Analyze BOSSA documented expenses, budgets, sales, deposits and margins; reconcile demo versus actual evidence and prepare financial owner decisions.
- `$bossa-staff-sops` — BOSSA Staff & SOPs: Prepare BOSSA training, staff-task plans, opening/closing service SOPs and handoffs using verified roles, schedules and controlled documents.
- `$bossa-digital-systems` — BOSSA Website & Internal OS: Route and inspect BOSSA public website versus internal Hospitality OS; prepare or implement scoped repository work, tests and protected acceptance evidence under owner gates.
- `$bossa-evidence-governance` — BOSSA Evidence & Decisions: Reconcile BOSSA conflicting records, source freshness, acceptance proof and precise owner approval scope; prepare decision packets and controlled evidence records.

Treat these as workflow skills, not autonomous agents. Use the current repository GPT directory for specialist expertise when accessible. Fire/kitchen covers FireGPT, MenuDesignGPT and SourBarGPT; inventory covers InventoryGPT/SupplierGPT; finance covers FinanceGPT/AnalyticsGPT; marketing covers MarketingGPT/ContentStudioGPT/YouTubeGPT/ViralGPT; guest/service covers ReservationGPT/GuestGPT/CustomerCareGPT/ServiceFlowGPT/LoyaltyGPT; staff covers TrainingGPT/OperationsGPT; catering covers EventPlannerGPT/PartnerGPT; owner control covers Bossa AI Ops/BossVision. Do not claim those GPTs were invoked.
