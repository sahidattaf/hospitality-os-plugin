# BOSSA Fire & Kitchen

Read [operating boundaries](references/operating-boundaries.md) and [source navigation](references/source-navigation.md) before producing material recommendations. Apply current owner authorization; the plugin itself grants no external action permission.

## Inputs

Use the owner request, exact action scope/gate, relevant dated source records and the domain inputs below. Complete supported work when inputs are missing; identify only the missing inputs needed for the remaining conclusion.

## Procedure and output

1. Identify controlled recipe/test ID, version, ingredients, allergens, intended portions and kitchen-verification status. Distinguish test planning from service prep; retrieve current phase.
2. Sequence bread tests (BREAD-001 then BREAD-002 if needed), component tests and box assembly. Log actual weights, usable yield, portions, cook observations, photographs, tasting feedback, defects and retest decision. Preserve failed cards and assign a new retest record.
3. Build measured prep quantities only from verified demand and approved recipes. Mark suggested batches as proposed; do not infer ready stock from a prep checklist or mark the fire station ready without a physical check.
4. Cover proteins, sauces/BOSSA JUS, sides, bread, fuel, trays, extraction and equipment checks. For drinks/Sour Bar distinguish recipe testing from licensed alcohol service. Retrieve current approved alcohol scope and licensing evidence before customer offers.
5. Address allergens and cross-contact explicitly. Do not guarantee allergen-free service without kitchen verification. Use current authoritative food-safety guidance for safety-critical temperatures/storage claims and cite it; require responsible kitchen sign-off for service readiness.

Return: test/prep sheet (ID/version, inputs, measured output, evidence, defect/retest, owner, next action); recipe approval packet and costing handoff. Do not convert proposed recipes or generated food imagery into authentic tested products.
