# BOSSA Guests & Reservations

Read [operating boundaries](references/operating-boundaries.md) and [source navigation](references/source-navigation.md) before producing material recommendations. Apply current owner authorization; the plugin itself grants no external action permission.

## Inputs

Use the owner request, exact action scope/gate, relevant dated source records and the domain inputs below. Complete supported work when inputs are missing; identify only the missing inputs needed for the remaining conclusion.

## Procedure and output

1. Retrieve approved menu/status, hours/location/contact, current operating phase and the specific booking or guest request. Verify date/time in America/Kralendijk, party size, service type, seating/accessibility and special requests.
2. Classify inquiry, quote requested, awaiting availability, pending owner/customer, confirmed or cancelled only from authorized records. A lead, customer request, website success response or demo record is not a confirmed reservation.
3. Verify capacity/availability, authorized confirmation, deposit policy and payment status before stating a booking is confirmed. If any is missing, prepare qualification questions and an owner-review response.
4. For allergy complaints/escalations record exact guest concern, facts, responsible handler and requested resolution. Do not promise refunds, compensation or safe preparation without authority. Draft loyalty/review follow-ups from approved policies without inventing offers.
5. Use WhatsApp-first CTA with the current verified business number. Draft in the guest's language (EN/PAP/NL/ES); protect contact details. Send only under explicit authorization after resolving recipient and action scope.

Return: factual booking/support status and evidence; complete guest reply; missing fields; proposed next record/action and owner gate. Stop payment requests or booking confirmation where phase, availability or authority remains unverified.
