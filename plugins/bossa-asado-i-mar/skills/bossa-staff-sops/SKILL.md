# BOSSA Staff & SOPs

Read [operating boundaries](references/operating-boundaries.md) and [source navigation](references/source-navigation.md) before producing material recommendations. Apply current owner authorization; the plugin itself grants no external action permission.

## Inputs

Use the owner request, exact action scope/gate, relevant dated source records and the domain inputs below. Complete supported work when inputs are missing; identify only the missing inputs needed for the remaining conclusion.

## Procedure and output

1. Read actual staff/role availability, training evidence, controlled SOP versions and current phase. Do not infer roles, rates or employment arrangements from a name or demo shift.
2. Prepare proposed assignments against required kitchen, service, cashier, cleaning and management responsibilities. Identify coverage gaps, dependencies and an accountable owner without inventing availability.
3. Author the requested SOP with purpose, scope, owner, inputs/equipment, sequential steps, measurable checks, exception/escalation, evidence record and version/review date. Mark drafts clearly; preserve controlled approved versions.
4. Cover opening readiness, fire prep, hygiene/allergens, guest handoff, incident intake, closing stock/cash checks and next-shift handoff where relevant. Route safety-critical specifics to verified current guidance and professional kitchen sign-off.
5. Link training tasks to acceptance proof: observed demonstration, completion record, supervisor sign-off and outstanding remediation. Do not mark trained or completed from a proposed checklist. For schedules or employment rules use actual availability and current applicable sources.

Return: scoped SOP or staffing/training plan; tasks (owner/date/dependency/evidence); coverage gaps; acceptance criteria; controlled-document and approval status. Do not issue staff messages, change rates/contracts or publish an approved SOP without authorization.
