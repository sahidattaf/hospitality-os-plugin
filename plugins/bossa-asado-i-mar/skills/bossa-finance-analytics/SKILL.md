# BOSSA Finance & Analytics

Read [operating boundaries](references/operating-boundaries.md) and [source navigation](references/source-navigation.md) before producing material recommendations. Apply current owner authorization; the plugin itself grants no external action permission.

## Inputs

Use the owner request, exact action scope/gate, relevant dated source records and the domain inputs below. Complete supported work when inputs are missing; identify only the missing inputs needed for the remaining conclusion.

## Procedure and output

1. Identify period, phase, entity, currency and source ledger/workbook/bank/invoice records. Classify actual, committed, forecast and demo amounts. Never report readiness rows as revenue or proof of live operations.
2. Reconcile invoice/payment/deposit IDs and dates. Avoid double counting transfers, deposits and final sales. Separate recognized revenue, cash receipts, cost of goods, operating expenses and capital rebuild expenditure according to verified accounting treatment.
3. Calculate only supported metrics: sales totals, food cost percentage, gross margin, budget versus actual and cash commitments. Provide formulas and denominator coverage; use Needs verification when figures are incomplete. Do not extrapolate profit from uncosted recipes.
4. Analyze variance drivers and dependency risks; label scenarios and missing data. For refund/billing actions verify policy, owner authority, original payment and customer identity. Do not execute payment/refund or change bank details under an analysis gate.
5. Use spreadsheet skills for workbook work. For financial/tax/legal guidance browse current authoritative sources and cite them, distinguishing assumptions from professional advice or recorded policy.

Return: period/currency/source coverage; reconciliation table; reproducible metrics with assumptions; up to three actions; decision packet; exact authorization boundary. Stop READY financial conclusions when material invoices, counts, policy or period evidence is missing.
