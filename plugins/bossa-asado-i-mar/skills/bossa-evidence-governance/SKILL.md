# BOSSA Evidence & Decisions

Read [operating boundaries](references/operating-boundaries.md) and [source navigation](references/source-navigation.md) before producing material recommendations. Apply current owner authorization; the plugin itself grants no external action permission.

## Inputs

Use the owner request, exact action scope/gate, relevant dated source records and the domain inputs below. Complete supported work when inputs are missing; identify only the missing inputs needed for the remaining conclusion.

## Procedure and output

1. Capture request, object/surface, permitted sources/tools, explicit action, owner gate and exclusions. Distinguish a skill installation/roster approval from authorization to execute downstream actions.
2. Build a source ledger: identity/link, version/date, retrieval time, domain authority, evidence label, claim and coverage. Separate snapshot, live read, owner statement, demo and proposed content.
3. Compare contradictions by domain. Code establishes implementation; permit evidence establishes permitted scope; menu master establishes approved business content. Flag stale/unknown facts and conflicts without inventing resolutions.
4. Assess exact acceptance criteria against actual proof. Use READY when all required evidence is verified, NOT READY for a verified failed criterion, NEEDS EVIDENCE for material missing proof, and BLOCKED for inaccessible required sources/actions. Explain scope and do not conflate structural validation, self-assessed synthetic tests, owner evidence and installed/live acceptance.
5. Produce a concrete approval packet: exact artifact/version/commit; requested action and surface; permitted tools/data; excluded actions; acceptance proof; risks/dependencies; stop conditions; rollback where applicable; owner decision fields. Approval drafts are not approvals.
6. Prepare a decision/evidence record; write to Notion or other systems only where authorized and verify the write. Preserve original records and failed test evidence.

Return: ledger/conflicts; scoped readiness decision; missing proof; approval packet or proposed decision record; maximum three safe next actions. Stop at the owner's exact boundary, not at a newly invented approval layer.
